
GHOST HUNTER 2 - The Boo-quel ????
For the Dick Smith VZ300 · ViBED by BPM 2026
Just in time for Halloween, the VZ gets the extravagant, mischievous Ghost Hunter sequel it always deserved!
The 1980s classic by Dubois & McNamara is back, and this time the ghosts have personalities:
GRUMBLE chases you and gets crosser as the maze empties.
SNEAKY cuts you off up ahead.
DIZZY wanders about… mostly.
PEEKABOO fades away until only his eyes are left.
Gobble pellets, grab a power pill and watch the whole screen flip as the hunted become the hunter. Chain your ghost-chomps for 200, 400, 800 and 1600 points. Eat all four in one go for a PERFECT, and the star appears that turns you into a flaming-capped super hunter.
Grab the power-ups along the way:
SPEED: zoom off and leave a trail.
FREEZE: put the ghosts on ice.
DECOY: leave a dummy for them to chase.
DOUBLE: twice the points.
SHIELD: shrug off one hit.
There are Halloween treasures too: candy, pumpkins, potions, skulls, gems and crowns.
You’ll get through six hand-built mazes with tunnels and teleporters, the frantic GHOST BUFFET and PELLET PANIC bonus rounds, and two cheeky intermissions. Meanwhile the difficulty climbs from friendly to delightfully chaotic.
It all wraps up in a proper arcade presentation: an animated graveyard title screen with lightning and thunder, a meet-the-ghosts screen, a power-up guide, a live demo, and a Hall of Fame with initials entry.
Happy haunting!
TECH SPECS
Hardware and loading
Machine: Dick Smith VZ300 (VZ200 compatible) with BennVenn SD loader and 128K RAM expansion. It needs RAM all the way up to $FFFF.
File: GHOST2.VZ, 18.6 KB, a machine-code (type F1) file that loads and runs at $8000.
Display
Graphics: Mode 1 (128×64, 4 colours) throughout, using both colour palettes to switch for power mode, lightning and flashes.
Rendering: flicker-free sprites drawn during the screen’s blanking period from a clean background copy. Sprites are pre-shifted for smooth one-pixel movement, and there are particle effects.
Timing: 50 fps, locked to the screen’s field-sync signal. There’s no ROM, no interrupts and no memory-banking.
Sound
Two-channel beeper music and effects, played in each frame’s spare time so gameplay never stalls.
Title theme plus jingles, a ghost siren, and about a dozen effects.
Game content
Six mazes (21×11 cells), with walls drawn at run time in five styles.
Two bonus rounds and two intermissions.
An AI-driven demo using the real game engine.
A five-entry high-score table with initials (kept until power-off).
Controls
Keyboard: Q/A/M/, (same as the original).
Joystick: either stick on the VZ joystick interface.
P pauses, S toggles sound, SPACE or fire starts.
Development
Source: Z80 assembly (sjasmplus) with Python asset tools.
Credits: original game by Dubois & McNamara; sequel ViBED by BPM 2026.
