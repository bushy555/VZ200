VZELDA: The Lost Princess.    (Brendan Murphy)

A Zelda-style dungeon adventure for the VZ300, vibe-coded entirely in Z80 assembly.
A dragon guards the Master Key to the princess’s prison. Fight through 12 rooms, find the bow, torch and magic wand, slay the dragon and set her free.

Features
12 rooms, with smooth eight-way scrolling in the larger ones
Torch-lit dark rooms where a flickering pool of light follows you
Sword, bow and magic wand
Five monster types and a three-phase dragon boss
Keys, secret walls, switches, crystal seals, traps and treasure
Joystick or keyboard control
34kb required
Tested on a VZ300
