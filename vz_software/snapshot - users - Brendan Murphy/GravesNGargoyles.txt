GRAVES ’N’ GARGOYLES
SIR TINPLATE’S LONG NIGHT
A gothic arcade platformer for the Dick Smith VZ300
VIBED BY BPM 2026 · Version 0.3.13
In the kingdom of Dreadmoor, the sorcerer Morgrave has stolen the seven Sunstones. The sun will not rise and the dead walk the land. Only one knight is still awake: Sir Tinplate, the least impressive knight in the realm, in borrowed tin armour. What could go wrong?
Fight through seven haunted stages, each with its own creatures, music and boss: Gallows Hill, the Witchwood, the Drowned Village, the Swamp of the Damned, the Crystal Deeps, the Demon Castle and, at last, Morgrave’s Citadel. Win better armour, from Undies and Tin up through Steel, Enchanted and Royal to Legendary, and watch it fly off piece by piece when you’re hit. Raid treasure chests for daggers, axes and torches, find the secret ledges, and bring back the dawn.
FEATURES
7 scrolling stages, 7 bosses and 49 creatures, each with its own behaviour. Zombies rise when you step near, skeletons fight on without their heads, phantom brides fade out and reappear behind you, stone gargoyles wake as you pass, magic mirrors fire back every time you throw, and more.
6 armour levels, drawn layer by layer on the knight. Each suit takes three knocks (watch the red health bar) before a piece flies off.
4 weapons:
The lance never runs out. Daggers, axes and torches come from chests with a limited supply of throws, shown on the HUD.
Find the same weapon again to enchant it: piercing lances, twin daggers, more axes and torches in the air.
Plus magic, charged by holding fire.
Mini-bosses, treasure chests, checkpoints, and a secret ledge on every stage from 2 to 7.
Two-channel beeper music: a gothic organ title theme, and a slow, suspenseful tune for every stage and boss.
Passwords for every stage, 3 difficulty levels (Casual, Normal, Expert), continues, a high-score table, and an attract mode with story, bestiary and a playing demo.
Keyboard or joystick.

TECH SPECS
Machine
Dick Smith VZ300 (Z80A, 3.55 MHz PAL)
Requires
BennVenn SD loader + 128K SRAM expansion
Display
Mode 1 (128 × 64, 4 colours); stages 5 and 7 use the second colour set (buff, cyan, magenta, orange)
Frame rate
About 25 fps during play
Scrolling
Smooth, 2 pixels at a time, through levels about 11 screens long
Video
Flicker-free and snow-free: each frame is built off-screen and copied to video RAM by the frame interrupt (IM2) during the vertical blank, using an unrolled stack copy
Sound
Two pulse-wave channels mixed on the 1-bit speaker at about 27.5 kHz, with pulse-width volume envelopes, plus sound effects. Music plays in the CPU’s spare time, so the in-game tunes are staccato and the title screen animates at half rate to give its tune room
Loading
GRAVGARG.VZ loads the engine; the title pack and each stage pack are read from the SD card as you go (under a second each)
Code
About 9,850 lines of hand-written Z80 assembly, plus about 8,000 lines of graphics, map and music data generated by Python tools
MEMORY USE
Main RAM, $7B00–$F9FF (31.75K usable; the BennVenn DOS owns $FA00 up)
