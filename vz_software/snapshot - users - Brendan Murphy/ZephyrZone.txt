# ZEPHYR ZONE
### An original pseudo-3D arcade shooter for the Dick Smith VZ300 · VIBED BY BPM 2026
Strap on the jet-pack and race over a checkerboard plain that rushes towards you out of the horizon.
Five worlds, each with its own palette, scenery and creatures, stand between ZEPHYR and the final
celebration. Formations swoop in and hold station in front of you, firing slow orbs that are aimed
at where you sit, so you have to keep moving. Shoot them down and they tumble to the ground in
flames. Trees, columns and towers can be blasted too, and every world ends in a boss fight.
Inspired by the Sharp MZ-700 conversion of Space Harrier, with all-new characters, art, music
and engine.
## The five worlds
| | World | Creatures | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **CHECKER PLAINS** | BLOBBIT, SPINDLE, HOPCAP | **COILWYRM**, a horned sky serpent |
| 2 | **SUNSET DUNES** | SANDRAY, SUNDISC, CACTOID | **SUN TOTEM**, a fiery carved face ringed by orbiting fire pods |
| 3 | **CRYSTAL NIGHT** | GLOWMOTH, PRISM, JELLYBELL | **SHARDWYRM**, a ten-segment crystal serpent |
| 4 | **NEON CITY** | DARTJET, COPSAUCER, GUNMECH (a walking gun robot) | **MEGAMECH**, a colossal robot head with gun pods |
| 5 | **MUSHROOM MARSH** | STONEFACE, BUZZWING, SNAILOPS (a giant cyclops snail) | **BOGAPEDE**, a twelve-segment centipede |
Beat all five for the finale: CONGRATULATIONS, fireworks, the cast gathered round Zephyr on his
podium, and the closing lines. Then round two begins, faster.
## Features
- **Perspective checkerboard ground**: 44 rows, every pixel offset pre-shifted, with haze at the
  horizon and parallax hills. Four ground styles: checker, dune ripples, seamed crystal tiles and a
  neon road.
- **Scaled sprites**: every creature, scenery item and boss is drawn at 11 sizes and 2 sub-positions,
  with bold MZ-700-style outlines, ground shadows and depth sorting.
- **Eight formation set pieces**: line, snake, ring, hop, swoop, dive, cross and spiral. Each formation
  holds station until it's shot down.
- **Speech**: "AAARGH!" when Zephyr is struck down and "GET READY!" as he gets back up, both sampled
  1-bit and played without warble.
- **Sound**: punchy one-bit effects (a crack-and-whistle for every kill). In play the effects alone
  make the soundscape; the title theme is written for the speaker time the engine has spare.
- **Attract sequence**: a live title with the demo pilot, a silent MZ-700-style picture title (Zephyr
  waving from a GUNMECH's shoulder while the logo rolls in), and a storyboard that parades each
  world's cast in turn.
- **Forgiving**: Casual, Arcade and Expert modes, a health bar on top of lives, continues, and a
  trainer that lets you start from any stage you've reached this session.
- **Controls**: keyboard, or a joystick through the Cybernoid/Redline port map.
## Tech specs
| | |
|---|---|
| Machine | Dick Smith VZ300 (Z80A, 3.5469 MHz), PAL |
| Needs | BennVenn SD card interface with 128K RAM expansion |
| Display | Mode 1, 128 x 64, 4 colours. Both palettes are used: CSS0 for green/yellow/blue/red worlds, CSS1 for buff/cyan/magenta/orange worlds |
| Frame rate | 16.7 frames a second (locked to 3 fields), triple-field timing with motion scaled by the fields elapsed |
| Rendering | Full-frame back buffer, copied to video RAM by an IM 2 field interrupt in two halves over two field gaps, so it never meets the beam (no snow) |
| Sprites | Colour-key records (4 pixels a byte), unrolled POP-driven blitter with clipping on all four sides |
| Projection | 1/z tables plus a quarter-square multiply. 56 depth buckets give painter's order |
| Sound | One-bit, one sample per two copied rows (3,233 Hz) plus the idle wait. Speech is 1-bit PCM at about 5,960 Hz with the CPU to itself |
| Language | 100% Z80 assembly. Assets are generated by Python tools (vector "puppet" art, font, tables, music) |
| Program size | 9,839 bytes .VZ; 151,151 bytes on SD in all (9 files) |
## RAM used
| Area | Used | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Main RAM $7C00-$B7FF | 14.5K | 9,815 bytes of code (incl. the "GET READY!" sample), 2.0K variables and 36 object slots, 2K back buffer, IM 2 table and stack |
| Video RAM $7000-$77FF | 2K | the screen |
| Expansion bank 3 ($4000-$67FF) | 10.0K (all) | projection and colour-key tables, the world's ground-strip library, the "AAARGH!" sample |
| Upper bank 0 ($B800-$FFFF) | 13.7K + DOS | front-end code (2.8K), message renderer buffers (1K), common sprites: hero, shots, explosions, flames, logo (9.9K); $F000-$FFFF left to the SD DOS |
| Upper bank 1 ($B800-$FFFF) | up to 17.0K | the current world (boss code, timeline, storyboard code, sprites, panorama, up to 16.3K) or the picture title or the finale, plus a 1K multiply table |
| **Total** | **about 57K** | of the VZ300's 18K plus the 128K expansion, with worlds streamed from SD as you reach them |
