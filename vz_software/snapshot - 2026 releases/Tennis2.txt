TENNIS II — GRAND SLAM
FOR THE DICK SMITH VZ200 / VZ300 — VIBED BY BPM 2026
New balls, please! The little green tennis court from the VZ is back for the big time. The original’s charm is all still here: you just run, and the racket swings itself. Where the ball meets you decides where it goes. This time there’s a whole world tour built around it.
Toss it up and time your serve: the higher you strike, the bigger the serve. Watch for faults, lets and aces. Hold left or right as you hit to steer the shot. Tap fire for a power drive, or SMASH! anything floating near the net. Pull back for a lob, or push up for a cheeky drop shot. The umpire calls every point, from FIFTEEN-LOVE through DEUCE to BREAK POINT and MATCH POINT. Hit three aces or winners in a row and you’re ON FIRE, with blazing power shots and a flame trail.
The World Tour takes you across four surfaces, each with its own colours and bounce:
VZ Open: fast, low grass
Paris Clay Cup: slow, high-bouncing orange clay
Hard Court Classic: cyan hard court
Night Masters: the blue indoor finale
Each tournament runs quarter-final, semi-final, final, with a trophy ceremony for the champion. Between tournaments there’s a Target Practice bonus round: a ball machine, twenty balls and three cones to hit. Win all four for the Grand Slam.
Eight rivals stand in your way, each with a portrait and a playing style:
Rookie Rusty
Lobby Lou
Net Netta
Big Serve Bazza
Dusty the Wall
Rocket Rita
The Professor
the champion himself, Mr VZ300
You can also play an Exhibition against anyone on any court, or go head to head in Two Players. Press C on the menu to unlock the Classic 1984 court, a loving recreation of the original’s stepped green court.
It all comes with:
a big-logo title screen with two little players rallying
a cheeky two-voice title theme, and jingles for every point, game, set and match
a satisfying racket thunk on every shot, and a big POP when you smash
a two-tier crowd of 54 little spectators in randomly mixed colours, who leap up after every point and do a Mexican wave for aces, winners and sets, with a real roar and crackling applause
WINNER!, ACE! and OUT! pop-ups, and the line judge’s flag
a ball with a shadow and a puff of dust on every bounce
blinds wipes between screens, and drifting grass or net dot textures behind every information screen
a full attract sequence with a computer v computer demo
a Hall of Fame with initials
SPECIFICATIONS
Machine
Dick Smith VZ300 or VZ200, Z80A at 3.5 MHz
Memory
Needs RAM from $7800 to $FFFF, e.g. a BennVenn SD + 128K expansion. Will not run on an unexpanded machine
RAM used
About 34 KB: all but 96 bytes of $7800–$FFFF. Code and data 24.7 KB, sprites built at run time 4.8 KB, court backdrop buffer 2 KB, and 2.4 KB for variables, draw queue, stack and crowd frames. Plus the 2 KB of video RAM, and a 64-byte save block in SRAM bank 3
Storage
Loads from SD (BennVenn loader) as a binary .VZ. No other files needed
File
TENNIS2.VZ, 24.7 KB, loads and autostarts at $8000
Display
Genuine Mode 1 throughout (6847 CG2: 128×64, 4 colours). Both colour sets are used: grass, night and title in CSS0, clay and hard court in CSS1
Graphics
Perspective court with a two-tier crowd stand and net. Pre-shifted, masked sprites, recoloured for each surface and when players change ends. 9 character portraits, title picture, trophy
Frame rate
50 fps, synced to field sync, with all screen writes in the vblank window (no snow)
Score panel
Framed panels for each player, with scores centred and in the player’s own colour
Effects
Two-frame animated two-tier crowd of 54 (bobbing and Mexican wave), bounce puffs, ball shadow, popups. Info screens have a drifting dot backplane (grass or net) that is drawn in vblank and steers clear of text
Sound
Two-voice beeper music (title theme, info shuffle, practice loop, Grand Slam march, 10 jingles) and 17 sound effects: a woody racket “thunk” for every strike, a loud pop for smashes, and white-noise crowd roars and applause. Music+SFX / SFX only / off
Controls
Keyboard: Q A M , to move, SPACE fire. Joysticks 1 and 2, read to suit the VZ joystick interface. Player 2 keys: I J K L + H. P pause, S sound
Modes
World Tour (1P), Exhibition (1P), Two Players, Target Practice, plus the hidden Classic 1984 court
Rules
Full tennis scoring: deuce/advantage, games, sets, tie-breaks, change of ends. Faults, double faults, lets and aces. Sets of 2, 4 or 6 games
Content
4 tournaments × 3 rounds. 8 computer opponents with their own speed, reach, reactions, accuracy, power, net play, serve and lobbing. 4 surfaces + Classic 1984, 20-ball target practice
Attract mode
Title, How to Play, Meet the Tour, computer v computer demo, Hall of Fame
High scores
Top 5 tour scores with three-letter initials. Kept in BennVenn SRAM bank 3 with a signature and checksum, so they survive reloading the game but not power-off
Exit
Reset or power-cycle
Language
100% Z80 assembly (sjasmplus-compatible), with Python asset and test tools
