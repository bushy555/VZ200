DAWN PATROL 2 for the Dick Smith VZ300 (BennVenn SD + 128K)
It’s 4:00 AM. Prisoners are held in four enemy camps, and only one chopper pilot can bring them home before 6:00.

The sequel to the 1985 VZ classic flies again with smooth parallax scrolling under a deep blue dawn sky, a sun that rises as the clock ticks, and a helicopter that dives, flares and turns to face you.
Fly four missions across Jungle, Desert, Highlands and City. Dodge tanks, jets, flak, homing SAMs, gunships and balloon mines, and steer clear of the freight planes you can’t shoot. Land inside the fence, hear the prisoners say “Hi!”, and race them back to the helipad before your fuel runs dry.
You get three-channel music, a full set of sound effects, armour, supply drops, a radar mini-map, a top-pilots high-score table and an attract demo. It plays with the keyboard or either joystick.
