VZ-TYPE? Is it a typing tutor? No! You can’t even get an AI to copy R-Type, so I had it make me my own instead!

VZ-TYPE is a brand-new horizontally scrolling shoot-’em-up for the Dick Smith VZ300, made for the BennVenn 128K RAM expansion and SD loader. I directed the design, playtested it on real hardware and called every tweak, from the bosses and power-ups down to the size of the hit box. The Z80 code, graphics and music were written to my direction by Claude, Anthropic’s AI. Everything in it is original: the code, the graphics, the eight stages, the bosses and the music. It’s a single .VZ file loaded straight from SD card, with no extra data files.
You pilot a lone fighter through eight stages of biomechanical trouble: riveted space-station hulls, pulsing organic hives, boulder-strewn caverns, crystal veins, gut-like tunnels and the deep field beyond. Each one has its own colours and textured scenery, with a smooth-scrolling 128×64 playfield over a parallax backdrop of distant skylines and drifting stars. Stage 5 is one long fly-past of a giant enemy dreadnought, where you weave between its decks to knock out turrets and pulsing reactor nodes. Every stage ends with a huge boss that fills the screen: an armoured fortress, a brood mother, a crystal colossus, the dreadnought’s bridge tower, a horned maw, a plated leviathan, a shelled overmind, and an armoured serpent that coils through the rocks until you’ve smashed every plate off its back.
Hold fire to charge a beam that ploughs through whole formations. Shoot down carriers for lettered power-ups. S, L and C arm your pod, a detachable orb that fires with you and can be launched ahead to clear the way or called back as a shield. + speeds up your ship, and H brings a pair of helper satellites that ride alongside and add their own firepower. Your current weapon, speed and helpers show in the status bar, and the ship banks and flares its engine as you fly.
It all runs at a steady 25 frames per second, with gritty beeper music on the title screens and plenty of in-game sound effects. The animated title sequence shows off the ship’s firepower, and there’s a hall of fame to fight your way onto. Keyboard (QAOP, SPACE, M) or joystick both work. If you just want to see the later stages, press T on the title for trainer mode with infinite lives (no high scores recorded).
