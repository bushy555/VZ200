
============
(Q) Serials
============
The serial number listing. Started off as an idea to collect known serials, and has grown.
It appears as though Laser500, Laser700 also use the same sequential serial numbers.
From a random source it mentioned that batches of numbers were produced at different processing plants.
However after browsing the listing there appears to be no real meaningful or identifiable groups of numbers,
other than the cut off periods of the 300 and 310 generations. 


All units have 2k video which is included in the RAM totals below.
4k = 2k user RAM + 2k video RAM.
8k = 6k user RAM + 2k video RAM.
18k= 16k user RAM + 2k video RAM.
66k= 64k user RAM + 2k video RAM.

Note:-
"Laser200 generation 2":  Seltron style layout of PCB, ie, Laser310 circuit board in Laser200 housing.

"VZ300/Laser310 generation 1": Brown keys with no under labels on keys.  mid 1985-1986.  (Late 1984 for Germany!)
"VZ300/Laser310 generation 2": Brown keys with under labels on keys.     1986-1987.
"VZ300/Laser310 generation 3": beige/cream keyboard.                     1987 onwards.

Known to exist units with missing serial numbers unfortunately.
Some will be impossible to further trace into the future.
