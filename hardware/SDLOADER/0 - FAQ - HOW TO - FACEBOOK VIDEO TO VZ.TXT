How to do a FACEBOOK video to VZ.   (Rough guide)
=================================================

Get Facebook video into MP4/AVI.
-------------------------------
Since facebook is next to useless in saving or grabbing or recording a posted video, need to take things into 
your own hands, by finding a cool freeware video grabber that will record straight to an AVI / MP4 video file.
A million utils come back when you do a google; most are utterly crapware in that they advertise as free and 
free download, and will install, but you go to run them and will want you to make payment. At this point, you
then find the contact details of the website, and fully *BLAST* them about their crapp crappy greedy money crap
software. Get stuck into them.
From memory Windows 10 and 11 might have its own screen recording software now. I try not to use Win 10/11 so
Im only having an educated guess here.  Find a screen recorder, Im sure that someone reputible such as NCH Software
might do a half decent recorder.
Find something that will work, installed. Select the area around the Facebook video post. Play the video and record it.
If the video plays two or three times as a loop, may as well try to record all two or three looped animations.


Extraction.
-----------
Do a few googles on online free MP4toBMP or AVItoVMP. You will eventually find one that will work for nix. 
There are thousands of these as well. Upload your AVI or MP4, and will be able to download an extraction of the
Facebook animation as a bunch of BMP files. Or PNG if you are that way inclinded.

Failing all of this, grab the GNU freeware util: FFMPEG.

From Ben's original doco:

Command line:

FFMPEG -i source.mp4 -r 12 -f image2 -vf format=gray -s 128x64 .\output\%d.bmp

>  r 12 = frame rate, 12 frames per second. Adjust to suit. 
>  This will be higer when I tidy up the code. Source can be a range of video formats. 
>  Make an ouput sub-directory.
>  After running this, you'll get hundreds/thousands of bitmap files in the output sub-folder.



Cleanup.
---------
Go through the tens/hundreds/thousands of BMP files, and mark the ones you want to keep for the animation.
Delete those that you don't want.
I mentioned to record a looped animation a few times; this is in case within the first loop a recorded 
split screen , or partial displayed screen, has been recorded. You will most likely be lucky to get a 
cleaner recorded , but same image, within the recorded second looped animation. If this is the case,
grab the same recorded BMP image from within the second looped lot of BMP's and replace it in the first 
lot of BMPs (...if aframe is split, or half recorded or something like it).

This process will perhaps take the most time.

Cleanup 2
---------
Depending on the number of images,  simple animation may have 20 to 50 frames.
Can easily handle manipulation with these numbers, such as a 128x96 frames and manually copy / paste 
a section from a specific X & Y axis into, say 128x64.  A few hundred images and copy/paste is perhaps 
getting  a bit silly.

May need to use Paint SHop Pro, to resize each frame. And/OR reduce the colour set by CTRL-SHIFT-6 . 
I forget the actual workflow process, but this can be done before or after Dithertron.   
In Paint Shop Pro, resize / recolour / resave-as Windows BMP.
Recolouring to 4 colour will put the colour pallet out-of-whack.
Down further below, oince the DAT file is created, and use 4-color-maniupulator to change colours 
within the outputed stacked DAT file. Works.  
16-bit Util: STRIP117.EXE


Use www.dithertron.com  (Google : "Dithertron") select 6847 and either 128x64 or 128x96 or 128x192.
Load in each frame image, and save as PNG.  Dithertron can clean up the recorded colours close to that 
of the 6847. 


STACKER
-------
Use the BMPtoJVD 4 colour Stacker.  This loads in all frames, sarting from 1.BMP, 2.BMP, 3.BMP etc and 
adds them together (stacks them) as one contiguous / continuous file.
COPY /B 1.BMP + 2.BMP + 3.BMP OUT.DAT  might also work. Maybe the BMP header will get in the way(?)


LOADER
-------
Simply use the AHAFAST.ASM loader. Change the inputted read filename.
Assemble to a .VZ file


OUTPUT & VZ
-----------
Copy the .VZ file and the .DAT file to a SD card, chuck SD card into the VZ SD Loader.
Boot, and then:
LOAD "FILE"			....sit back and watch.


Colours
---------
Then you work out that the colours are all wrong, or yellow & blue need to be swapped.
This is where the 4-colour-manipulated util will work to move/swap/change colours within the 
video DAT file, from one colour to another.
To do:  make or allow for a fifth temp colour so that individual specific colours can be moved.

After swapping colours, recopy, re-watch.
